Ishta Account Deletion

Last updated: July 27, 2026

You can delete your Ishta account from inside the Ishta app.

Delete Your Account In The App

  1. Open Ishta.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to your profile and open account or security settings.
  4. Choose the account deletion option and confirm the request.

What Deletion Does

When your account is deleted, Ishta revokes active sessions, deletes your authentication account, private Feed impression and interaction history, Feed Not interested exclusions, and account-owned avatar and post image objects from active private and public storage. Ishta purges public cache entries under its control. Copies previously downloaded, cached, linked, or indexed by third parties may remain outside Ishta's control. Some records may be deleted, anonymized, or retained when needed for security, integrity, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups, or to preserve non-personal aggregate metrics. Encrypted backup copies may persist for up to 30 days before deletion.

Request Deletion Outside The App

If you cannot access the app, email team@joinishta.app from the email address associated with your Ishta account and ask to delete your account.

We may need to verify that you control the account before completing the request.
