Ishta Community Guidelines

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Ishta is for rating food with people you trust. These guidelines explain what is not allowed and how safety tools work.

Contact: team@joinishta.app

Not Allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, targeted abuse, or threats.
  • Hate or discrimination based on protected or personal characteristics.
  • Sexual content, nudity, or exploitative content.
  • Graphic violence or encouragement of self-harm.
  • Spam, scams, deceptive behavior, or platform manipulation.
  • Private or sensitive information about another person.

Reports

You can report posts, comments, and profiles from their action menus. Reporting hides the item for you and sends it to Ishta for review.

Blocks

Blocking removes existing follows and follow requests between you and the blocked user. You and that user will no longer be able to see or interact with each other in Ishta.

Review

Ishta reviews reported content and may dismiss the report, remove content, restrict an account, or take other action needed to protect the service.
